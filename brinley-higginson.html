Brinley Higginson

CSWi

Brinley is currently pursuing a Master of Social Work from Utah State University, after graduating with her Bachelor of Social Work also from Utah State University in 2025. She is a proud Snow College alumni. She has a passion for trauma informed care, equine assisted services, supporting individuals and families, and behavioral support.

Brinley has experience working with children and adolescents, behavioral concerns, family relationships, and college students. She is interested in working in equine assisted services someday. She believes that every individual and family has unique strengths and experiences, and that counseling can provide a supportive environment to explore challenges, develop skills, and build healthier relationships. She is committed to ongoing education and professional development to expand her knowledge of various therapeutic modalities and theories.

In working with clients, Brinley strives to create an environment that is compassionate, nonjudgmental, safe, supportive, and strengths based. She believes in meeting clients where they are and working collaboratively to identify goals that are meaningful to them.

Outside of work, Brinley enjoys spending time with family, being outdoors, photography, reading, and going on adventures with her husband.
