Adriana Casillas, CSW

Payson Counseling Center

Adriana Casillas, CSW, earned her Master of Social Work degree from Utah Valley University. She enjoys working with children, adolescents, individuals with autism and developmental disabilities, and families from immigrant communities. Her approach is rooted in person-centered and strengths-based principles, incorporating play-based interventions and evidence-informed strategies tailored to each client’s individual needs.

Adriana believes clients are the experts in their own lives. She approaches therapy as a collaborative process, helping clients recognize their strengths, navigate challenges, and explore new perspectives. She values genuine connections and believes in creating a space where clients feel heard while also being encouraged to grow. Fluent in both English and Spanish, Adriana recognizes the importance of culture, identity, and personal experiences in shaping each individual’s journey.

Outside of work, Adriana enjoys spending time with friends and family, camping, hiking, listening to Zach Bryan, and finding any excuse to shop for or redecorate her home.
