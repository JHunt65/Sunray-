Becky Dalton

LCSW

Becky holds a Master’s Degree in Social Work from the University of Utah and has training in CBT, child-centered play therapy, and Jungian therapy. Her experience includes school-based mental health coordination and domestic violence advocacy.

She is passionate about creating safe spaces for healing, especially for children. Becky enjoys farm life, local rodeos, Southern Utah, and time with family and friends.
