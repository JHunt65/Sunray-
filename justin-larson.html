Justin Larson

Ephraim Counseling Center

Justin is currently pursuing his Master of Science in Clinical Mental Health Counseling at Rocky Mountain University of Health Professions. He earned his Bachelor’s degree in Psychology from Utah Valley University and is passionate about helping individuals navigate life's challenges and discover their strengths.

Justin takes a person-centered approach to counseling, believing that clients are the experts in their own lives. He strives to create a supportive, collaborative environment where clients feel heard, valued, and empowered to guide the direction of their therapeutic journey. His goal is to help clients identify the tools, skills, and insights needed to foster meaningful growth and lasting change.

A proud Sanpete County native, Justin has spent the past five years working with youth and families in school settings and adolescent behavioral treatment centers. He especially enjoys working with adolescents and adults and is committed to helping clients build resilience, overcome obstacles, and reach their full potential.

Outside of the counseling office, Justin enjoys spending time in the mountains with his family, exploring the outdoors, and keeping up with the latest Star Wars adventures. His down-to-earth personality and genuine passion for helping others allow him to connect with clients in a meaningful and authentic way.
