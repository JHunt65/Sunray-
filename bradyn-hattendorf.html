Bradyn Hattendorf

CSW

Bradyn is a Certified Social Worker (CSW) working toward LCSW licensure. He loves working with youth and adults in rural Utah and strives to create a welcoming space where clients feel heard, respected and supported. He specializes in domestic violence and cares deeply about supporting people experiencing suicidal thoughts. He approaches these experiences with compassion and without judgment. He helps clients build safety, connection and hope.

His approach is collaborative and tailored to each person's needs. He draws from Cognitive Behavioral Therapy (CBT), Dialectical Behavior Therapy (DBT), family systems, and solution-focused approaches. This helps clients build practical coping skills, strengthen relationships, and work towards meaningful change.
