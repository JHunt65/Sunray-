About Sunray Family Counseling

We are a dedicated counseling group practice offering therapy for individuals of all ages, with specialized expertise in trauma therapy and support for children and adolescents. We have locations in Cedar City, Ephraim, Payson, and Richfield, and also provide convenient telehealth options. Our commitment to serving rural and underserved populations is at the heart of what we do.

Our experienced therapists are trained in a variety of therapeutic modalities, including play therapy, sand tray therapy, and more. We are passionate about providing the highest quality care and are committed to continuous learning and ongoing training to ensure that our clients receive the best possible treatment.

Why should I consider therapy?

Everyone can benefit from therapy, as it provides a space to be seen, heard, and to build positive resources. Therapy can be both rewarding and challenging, offering valuable insights into your personal growth and well-being. People seek therapy for a variety of reasons, including:

  • Reducing stress, anxiety, and depression
  • Improving interpersonal relationships and communication
  • Gaining comfort in social, work, and family settings
  • Increasing self-compassion and self-acceptance
  • Developing solutions to specific problems
  • Gaining a better understanding of personal goals and values
  • Processing and understanding traumatic experiences

Personal and relational growth requires significant effort and commitment. Honesty and a willingness to change behaviors, thoughts, and feelings are essential in fostering positive change both individually and within relationships.

What can I expect during the therapeutic process?

Engaging in therapy can be incredibly rewarding, but it may also present challenges. You may experience emotions such as anger, sadness, or fear during the process. These feelings are natural and part of the healing journey. Some aspects of personal growth may come quickly, while other parts may take time and feel frustrating.

The therapeutic process may also lead to unanticipated changes, such as shifts in personal goals or relationships. It is important to remember that you are the ultimate decision-maker when it comes to your personal life. We encourage you to weigh the potential benefits and risks of therapy. For many, the benefits far outweigh the challenges, helping them move toward positive change.

What types of therapy do you offer?

Sunray Family Counseling provides therapy for a multitude of issues. All of our therapists have some training in play therapy as well as experiential client-centered care. We have many clinicians who are trained in trauma-informed approaches such as EMDR and ART. We strive to maintain evidence-based approaches and work to match you to a therapist with a theoretical approach that will best serve you.

We offer a wide range of mental health services including therapy for children, adolescents, and adults. We provide EMDR and ART.

How long does therapy last?

The duration of treatment will depend upon your progress and the specifics of your situation. Either you or your therapist may terminate treatment at any time.

What if I do not like my therapist?

We understand that the therapeutic relationship is one of the biggest predictors of success in therapy. Finding the right fit with your therapist is an important part of the process, and sometimes it may take a little time. We recommend giving your therapist a couple of sessions to see if the connection clicks. However, if you feel the fit is not right, please do not hesitate to let your therapist or our office know. We can assist in finding a new therapist for you.

If you have any concerns or complaints, please feel free to reach out to our office or email grievances@sunrayfamilycounseling.com. We are committed to ensuring you feel comfortable and supported throughout your therapeutic journey.

What is the cost of therapy?

At Sunray Family Counseling, we accept various forms of payment, including checks, debit cards, credit cards, HSA/FSA, ecclesiastical pay, and cash. We also accept insurance, and for those paying out of pocket or out of network, we offer discounts. For more specific pricing information, please contact our office.

We understand that financial concerns can sometimes be a barrier, and we are committed to working with you to ensure therapy is accessible while maintaining fairness for both clients and therapists. If you have any questions or need assistance with payment options, please do not hesitate to reach out to our office.

Do you take insurance?

Yes, we work with a variety of insurance companies, and we understand that there are both benefits and limitations to using insurance for mental health services. Some insurance providers may have limited mental health options, dictate the number of sessions, or require a diagnosis.

At this time, we are an in-network provider with the following:

  • Select Health
  • BCBS
  • Tricare
  • TriWest
  • PEHP
  • EMI
  • UMR
  • EMBS
  • Motive Health
  • University of Utah
  • Aetna
  • VA
  • Medicare
  • Medicaid*

*Note: We are not a community provider for Medicaid, but we can see some DHS Medicaid plans for foster children, post-adopt, and JJYS services. We are not contracted with traditional Medicaid and cannot provide services under that plan.

We also have contracts with several Employee Assistance Programs (EAPs).

For DMBA and Cigna, we are out-of-network providers but can process claims at a reasonable rate. For all other insurance providers, we are out-of-network, but we are happy to provide documentation for reimbursement. However, each insurance company processes reimbursements differently, and you will be responsible for ensuring you understand your reimbursement process.

Important note: While we make every effort to verify your benefits, you are ultimately responsible for understanding what your insurance provider covers. Your mental health benefits may be managed separately from your medical benefits, and there may be a deductible that is different from your medical deductible. Some insurance providers also use third parties to handle mental health benefits. We strongly encourage all clients to verify their benefits directly with their insurance provider.

Are therapy sessions confidential?

Therapy is designed to be a safe place where you are free to talk about any personal issues and concerns you may have. Our therapists respect the need for privacy and adhere to strict confidentiality policies. The things you discuss will be private and will not be disclosed to anyone.

There are, however, situations in which therapists are legally required to pass information on. These include:

  • Disclosures of intent to harm self or others
  • Suspected abuse or neglect of a child or dependent adult
Do you provide letters or accommodations?

At Sunray Family Counseling, we do not guarantee the fulfillment of requests for letters or accommodations. This includes disability paperwork, work or school accommodations, or other similar requests. Each request will be evaluated on a case-by-case basis, and will only be considered if the therapist and client have developed an adequate therapeutic relationship and the therapist has a full understanding of the client's mental health needs.

We do not provide letters for emotional support animals or for court/legal matters. Additionally, no therapist at Sunray is trained as a custody evaluator, and we will not provide documentation or advocacy in custody matters.

Do you provide custody evaluations or participate in legal proceedings?

At Sunray Family Counseling, our primary focus is on your therapeutic well-being. We do not provide custody evaluations or recommendations regarding custody arrangements. For custody-related matters, you may be referred to an independent custody evaluator. For more information about our approach to custody issues, please refer to our informed consent.
