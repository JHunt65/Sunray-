Lexi Murray

MSWi Intern Therapist | Ephraim Counseling Center

Lexi is an MSWi currently completing her practicum with Boise State University for her Master of Social Work. She earned her Bachelor of Social Work at Utah State University, where she interned with the Division of Child and Family Services as a caseworker.

Grounded in Trauma-Focused CBT techniques, Adlerian approaches, and child-centered play therapy, Lexi believes in meeting clients where they are and helping them feel connected to themselves and recognize their worth.

An Arizona native and soon-to-be mom, Lexi enjoys hiking, crafting, and life in Utah with her firefighter husband and dog, Smokey.
