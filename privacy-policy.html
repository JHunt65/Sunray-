Privacy Policy

How Sunray Family Counseling handles website visitor information.

Overview

Sunray Family Counseling values the privacy of every visitor. This policy explains what website information may be collected, how it may be used, and what choices visitors have when interacting with our site.

If you have privacy-related questions, please contact our office directly and we will do our best to assist you.

Website Data and Log Information

Like most websites, we may collect basic technical information when visitors browse our pages. This may include IP address, browser type, internet service provider, timestamps, referring and exit pages, and click activity.

This information is used for site administration, trend analysis, performance monitoring, and improving visitor experience. We do not use this technical data by itself to identify individual users.

Cookies and Similar Technologies

Our website may use cookies to remember preferences, improve usability, and help pages load and function properly.

You can control or disable cookies through your browser settings. Limiting cookies may affect how some parts of the website work.

Advertising and Third-Party Services

Some third-party services linked from or integrated with our website may use their own technologies such as cookies, scripts, or web beacons.

Sunray Family Counseling does not control third-party privacy practices. We encourage visitors to review the privacy policies of any external providers they interact with.

Third-Party Privacy Policies

This policy applies to Sunray Family Counseling's website only. It does not apply to other websites, advertisers, or services accessible through external links.

For details about how those third parties collect or process data, please review their individual policies.

Children's Privacy

Protecting children online is important to us. We do not knowingly collect personally identifiable information from children under 13 through this website.

If you believe a child has provided personal information through our site, contact us promptly so we can review and remove the information when appropriate.

Online Scope

This Privacy Policy describes website-related privacy practices only. It does not govern information collected offline or through channels outside this website.

Consent

By using this website, you acknowledge this Privacy Policy and agree to the terms described here, along with our Terms & Conditions.
