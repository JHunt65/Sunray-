Rachel Allen

Founder

Rachel is the founder and original owner of Sunray Family Counseling Center and loves to continue the legacy of what she has worked so hard to build.

She is an LCSW with her Master of Social Work from Brigham Young University and her Bachelors from the University of Southern California. She is an RPT and RPT-S and has extensive training in EMDR, playful EMDR, ART, play therapy, and Jungian Play Therapy.

She has lived in a variety of places and loves exploring with her husband and 3 children.
