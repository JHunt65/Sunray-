Renee Madsen

Billing Director

Renee has been the Billing Director at Sunray for 10½ years. She manages billing and tracking for grants, state organizations, Crime Victim services, Employee Assistance Programs, Workers’ Compensation, and other mental health therapy payers. She loves being part of Sunray’s family-like environment and knowing that her work helps support her community.

Family is the most important part of Renee’s life. She is married and has six children, 28 grandchildren, and five great-grandchildren. She enjoys gardening, walking with friends, spending time outdoors with her husband and family, and cheering on her grandchildren at sporting events and dance performances. Renee also has a playful Siamese kitten named Poppsi. Her faith and religious service are important parts of her life and help her grow in love and service to others.
