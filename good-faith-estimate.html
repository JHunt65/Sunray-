Good Faith Estimate

Estimated costs for services before you receive care.

What to expect

Sunray Family Counseling provides a Good Faith Estimate to help families understand the expected costs of counseling and support services when they do not have insurance coverage or when services are not fully covered.

Estimate components

  • Initial intake and assessment
  • Individual counseling sessions
  • Family and caregiver support sessions
  • Behavioral planning and care coordination

Need help with your estimate?

Contact our office for a personalized Good Faith Estimate based on your family’s needs and the services being recommended.
