Our Services

Therapy and support tailored to your family’s needs.

Sunray Family Counseling offers compassionate, evidence-based care for children, teens, adults, couples, families, and groups across southern Utah.

Play Therapy

Creative, play-based counseling helps children express emotions, build resilience, and work through difficult experiences in a safe setting.

EMDR

Eye Movement Desensitization and Reprocessing supports healing from trauma, anxiety, and distressing memories with a proven clinical approach.

Experiential Therapy

Hands-on methods such as art and sand tray interventions help clients access deeper insight and process emotions through experience.

Child Therapy

Specialized treatment for children uses developmentally appropriate tools, skill-building, and support to address behavior, trauma, and emotional growth.

Adolescent Therapy

Teen-focused counseling supports healthy identity, coping skills, and emotional resilience during a time of change and challenge.

Adult Therapy

Individual therapy helps adults address anxiety, depression, relationship struggles, and life transitions with clarity and support.

Couples Therapy

Couples sessions strengthen communication, rebuild trust, and help partners work together toward shared goals.

Family Therapy

Family-focused care helps relatives build better understanding, healthier boundaries, and stronger support systems.

Group Therapy

Small groups provide a safe space for people to share, learn, and grow alongside others facing similar challenges.

If you have questions about services or want help choosing the right fit, our team is ready to listen and guide your next step.
